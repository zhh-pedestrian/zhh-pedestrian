## 👋 About Me

A passionate bioinformatician focused on unraveling complex genomic and transcriptomic data to advance our understanding of hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). I combine rigorous data analysis with creative problem-solving to extract meaningful insights from biological sequences.

**Research Interests:**
- 🔬 Transcriptomics & Genomics (scRNA-seq, ncRNA, cell-free eccDNA)
- 🎯 Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC) - mechanisms and biomarkers
- 💾 Genomic Databases (NGDC, GeneCards, TCGA)
- 📊 Computational Analysis & Data Visualization
